Hello! I am Ziqi, a PhD student at [Caltech](https://www.cms.caltech.edu). My goal is to let AI understand and act in the physical world. Currently I do this via "world modeling'' -- letting AI learn physical priors by generating and modeling the world. I am advised by [Georgia Gkioxari](https://gkioxari.github.io/) and [Yisong Yue](http://www.yisongyue.com/). I interned at [World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai) and [Meta SuperIntelligence Labs](https://ai.meta.com/research/). I am also a [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/) and [UChicago](https://www.uchicago.edu) alumni.
